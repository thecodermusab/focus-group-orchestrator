# Campaign Focus-Group Report

**Product brief:** SmartBrew: an AI-powered coffee machine that learns your schedule and brews before you wake up.
**Platform:** Instagram
**Status:** approved after 2 round(s)

## Winning ad

> **Fresh coffee, zero effort — and zero waste.**
> SmartBrew learns your schedule, brews before you wake, and its compostable pods cut waste by 80%. Free shipping this week.

*Angle:* convenience + sustainability (revised per focus group) — *mean purchase intent:* 8.5/10

## Decision log

### Round 1: REVISE
- Selected: A | Dropped: B, C
- Personas routed to next: genz, elderly
- Rationale: Draft A scored highest overall but Gen-Z (4/10) and the elderly persona (3/10) rejected it; the professional persona is already convinced, so only the two dissatisfied personas need to re-evaluate the revision.
- Revision instructions: Keep the convenience hook but add a sustainability angle for Gen-Z and simpler, concrete language with a price/offer signal for the elderly customer.

### Round 2: APPROVE
- Selected: A2 | Dropped: —
- Personas routed to next: — (none / all satisfied)
- Rationale: All consulted personas now report purchase intent >= 8; conversion goal met.
